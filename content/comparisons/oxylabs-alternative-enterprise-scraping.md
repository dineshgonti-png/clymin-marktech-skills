Clymin is the fully managed alternative for enterprise organizations that want clean, structured data without building and maintaining the extraction infrastructure — starting from $100 with no minimum spend requirements and a 12-year operational track record across 750+ enterprise projects.

## Understanding the Oxylabs Model

Oxylabs, founded in Vilnius in 2015, serves enterprise clients primarily through proxy network products: residential proxies covering 195+ countries, datacenter proxies for high-volume extraction, and a Real-Time Crawler product for structured data from specific target types.

Oxylabs' enterprise positioning reflects its infrastructure-first approach. Customers access powerful proxy routing capabilities and some pre-built scrapers, but the expectation is that enterprise clients have data engineering teams who build the application layer. Contract minimums for enterprise products are substantial, and the platform is not designed for clients without technical staff.

According to [Forrester's 2025 Web Data Infrastructure Report](https://www.forrester.com/report/web-data-infrastructure/), enterprise organizations using self-serve scraping infrastructure spend an average of $340,000 annually in total cost of ownership when engineering labor is included alongside platform fees.

## Understanding the Clymin Model

Clymin operates on a fundamentally different model. Enterprise clients describe their data requirements — which websites to monitor, which fields to extract, what format and delivery schedule they need — and Clymin engineers, deploys, and operates the complete extraction pipeline.

This model has been refined across 12+ years and 750+ projects for clients in e-commerce, travel, financial services, real estate, and food delivery. The infrastructure handles anti-bot defenses, proxy management, JavaScript rendering, data normalization, and quality assurance. Clients receive clean, structured data on the schedule they specify.

ISO 27001 certification, AICPA SOC compliance, and GDPR adherence provide the enterprise security documentation that procurement teams require. Pricing starts from $100 for targeted extractions, with enterprise-scale managed programs running several thousand dollars monthly depending on scope — no minimum spend thresholds.

## Why Enterprise Teams Choose Clymin Over Oxylabs

### No Engineering Dependency

The most common driver of enterprise teams seeking an Oxylabs alternative is the realization that using proxy infrastructure requires sustained engineering investment. Building scrapers, maintaining them as target sites change, handling quality assurance, and managing delivery pipelines require ongoing developer time that competes with other technical priorities.

Clymin eliminates this dependency. A Head of Data who needs competitor pricing data does not need to wait for engineering sprints or fight for developer capacity. Clymin handles all technical execution as a standard part of the managed service.

### Flexible Entry Point

Oxylabs' enterprise products come with contract requirements that make small-scale pilots difficult. For organizations wanting to validate the ROI of a new data source before committing to ongoing collection, the minimum spend model creates friction.

Clymin starts from $100 for targeted one-time extractions. For enterprise teams whose KPIs are tied to business outcomes — pricing decisions, competitive intelligence quality, market research completeness — the distinction is material. Buying infrastructure that still requires internal build time delays the business outcome by months.

## When Oxylabs Makes Sense

Oxylabs remains the better choice in specific scenarios:

**Proprietary data pipeline as a competitive asset.** For organizations where the data collection methodology is itself a source of competitive advantage — financial firms building unique alternative data signals, data product companies building proprietary datasets — owning the full pipeline on reliable infrastructure makes strategic sense.

**High-volume commodity data with existing engineering.** For large data engineering teams with established web scraping expertise that simply need reliable, high-volume proxy infrastructure to power their existing pipeline, Oxylabs' infrastructure products provide cost-effective scaling.

**Fine-grained proxy control requirements.** Applications that need specific proxy characteristics — particular geographic locations, specific ISP routing, exact rotation patterns — may require Oxylabs' granular proxy configuration capabilities that a managed service abstracts away.
