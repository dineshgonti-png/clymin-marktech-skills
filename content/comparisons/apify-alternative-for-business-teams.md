Apify is a cloud-based developer platform for building and running web scraping workflows. Clymin fills that gap — a fully managed scraping service where business teams describe what data they need, and Clymin delivers it within 48 hours without requiring a single line of code from the client.

## What Is Apify?

Apify is a Prague-based cloud platform founded in 2015 that lets developers build, deploy, and run web scraping programs called Actors. The platform provides cloud infrastructure, a JavaScript/Python SDK for building Actors, and a marketplace of pre-built Actors covering common scraping use cases.

Apify's model serves developers and technical teams well. The Actor architecture is flexible, the platform handles scheduling and compute scaling, and the marketplace reduces time-to-value for standard use cases. For teams with JavaScript or Python developers who want control over their extraction logic, Apify is a capable tool.

The platform's limitation for business teams is the same as most developer-facing tools: meaningful customization requires code. Pre-built marketplace Actors cover a finite set of targets and extraction schemas. When an e-commerce team needs data from a custom competitor set, a specific product taxonomy, or structured delivery to their BI tools, they need developer involvement to make Apify work.

[BuiltWith's 2025 Web Technology Report](https://builtwith.com/reports/web-scraping-tools-2025) found that Apify adoption is concentrated in companies with in-house development teams, while business teams in e-commerce and marketing more frequently adopt managed service alternatives.

## When Apify Is the Right Tool

Apify outperforms managed services in specific scenarios:

**Developer-led projects with custom logic requirements.** If your team has developers and needs to build proprietary extraction logic that functions as a core technical asset, Apify's Actor framework provides the control and flexibility a managed service cannot match.

**Rapid prototyping of new data sources.** For a developer who wants to test whether a new data source contains useful signal before committing to ongoing extraction, Apify's marketplace of pre-built Actors enables fast experimentation.

**Integration with existing developer workflows.** If your data engineering team already uses JavaScript or Python extensively and wants scraping as a component in a larger custom pipeline, Apify integrates naturally with those workflows.

## Pricing: What Apify Actually Costs at Scale

Apify's pricing starts at a free tier with limited compute, progressing to paid plans from $49/month. However, the listed plan prices cover only a fixed amount of compute units. Large-scale e-commerce scraping consumes compute units quickly, with costs that scale significantly with volume.

Total monthly cost for a business team realistically runs $500-$3,000 for Apify plus $3,000-$8,000 equivalent in developer time.

Clymin handles the same scope for $2,000-$4,000/month with zero additional engineering overhead. For teams without dedicated scrapers developers, the managed approach is meaningfully cheaper on a total cost basis.
