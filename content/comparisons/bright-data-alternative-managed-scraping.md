## What Is Bright Data?

Bright Data (formerly Luminati Networks) is one of the world's largest web data infrastructure providers. Founded in 2014, the company operates a proxy network spanning 72 million residential IPs and offers several products including the Web Unlocker (for bypassing anti-bot systems), the Scraping Browser (a managed headless browser API), and a marketplace of pre-built datasets.

Bright Data's model is developer-centric. Customers access the platform via API or SDK, write their own scraping code, and use Bright Data's infrastructure to route requests and bypass blocks. The company charges primarily per gigabyte of proxy traffic consumed.

This model works well for engineering teams with web scraping expertise who want reliable infrastructure without operating their own proxy network. It is not designed for business teams who want data without building for it.

According to [G2's 2025 Web Scraping Category Report](https://www.g2.com/categories/web-scraping), Bright Data leads in infrastructure capabilities but scores significantly lower on ease of use and non-technical accessibility compared to managed service providers.

## When to Choose Bright Data

Bright Data is the right tool in specific situations:

**Your team has dedicated data engineers.** If you have staff with strong Python/JavaScript skills and web scraping experience, Bright Data gives you infrastructure control and flexibility to build exactly the extraction logic your use case requires.

**You need a proprietary data edge.** When differentiated data collection methodology is part of your competitive moat — a unique signal that competitors cannot replicate from a shared service — owning the scraping infrastructure makes strategic sense.

**Proxy infrastructure is the specific gap.** If you have existing scrapers and just need better proxy rotation and anti-bot bypass, Bright Data's Web Unlocker or residential proxy network may be the specific component you need without requiring a full managed service.

**You are building a data product.** Companies whose core business is selling data products may need direct infrastructure control rather than relying on a service provider.

## When to Choose Clymin
