## What Apify Provides

Apify is a cloud-based web scraping platform. It offers a marketplace of pre-built scrapers called "actors" for common targets (Amazon, Shopify stores, Google Shopping), proxy infrastructure for anti-bot navigation, and a development environment for building custom actors in Node.js or Python.

For ecommerce teams with engineering resources, Apify accelerates the build phase of a scraping program. Pre-built actors for Amazon and major Shopify stores reduce setup time versus building scrapers from scratch. The platform handles proxy rotation and cloud computing infrastructure.

The limitations: actors require engineering attention when target sites update their structure. Amazon changes its product page layout and anti-bot protections multiple times per year. Each update breaks existing actors and requires engineering time to fix. Pre-built actors from the marketplace vary in reliability and maintenance frequency from their creators.

## When Apify Makes Sense

Apify is appropriate for ecommerce teams with dedicated engineering staff, highly custom extraction requirements that don't fit standard competitive monitoring patterns, or use cases requiring deep product customization of extraction logic that managed services don't accommodate.

Teams building proprietary data products — extracting ecommerce data to sell or incorporate into a platform — often need the configurability that a self-service platform like Apify provides. Standard competitive price monitoring programs don't have these requirements.
