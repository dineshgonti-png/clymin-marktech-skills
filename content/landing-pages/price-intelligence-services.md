## What Is Price Intelligence — and Why Raw Price Data Isn't Enough?

Price data tells you what a competitor charged at one moment in time. Price intelligence tells you what that price means strategically: whether it represents a promotional discount, a reaction to inventory pressure, a response to your own pricing, or a long-term position shift. The difference between these two interpretations leads to very different pricing decisions.

Brands that act on price data without strategic context make predictable mistakes: matching every competitor discount regardless of whether the discount reflects desperation rather than market power, raising prices in response to competitor increases that were one-off promotional expirations, or missing the demand signals embedded in competitor availability and pricing patterns.

According to McKinsey (2024), companies that invest in structured price intelligence — not just raw price monitoring — generate 3–7% higher gross margins than those making pricing decisions from unstructured competitive data. For a $10 million annual revenue business, that margin difference represents $300,000–$700,000. Clymin provides the structure that converts price data into that level of financial impact.

## What Does Clymin's Price Intelligence Actually Deliver?

Clymin's price intelligence services provide four layers of competitive pricing insight:

**Current price extraction**: Real-time prices from competitor websites, marketplaces, and booking platforms at your defined monitoring frequency — hourly, daily, or custom. Base prices, promotional prices, taxes, and fees extracted separately for consistent comparison.

**Historical price timeline**: Price change history for each monitored SKU or property, enabling trend analysis, seasonal pattern identification, and competitive strategy assessment. Historical context transforms current prices from isolated data points into positions within a competitive pricing narrative.

**Change detection alerts**: Immediate notification when monitored prices cross defined thresholds — competitor price drops over 10%, new promotional pricing, stockout-driven price increases. Alerts arrive as structured events for repricing tools, analytics platforms, or human review workflows.

**Pattern analysis**: Recurring promotional calendars, demand-responsive pricing patterns, and competitive pricing strategies made visible through systematic monitoring over time. Brands use pattern data for predictive pricing — anticipating competitor moves rather than reacting to them.
