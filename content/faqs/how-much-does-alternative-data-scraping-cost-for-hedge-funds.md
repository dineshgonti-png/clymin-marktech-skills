## What Does Alternative Data Scraping Actually Cost?

The alternative data market has a wide price spectrum, and for good reason. A one-time extraction of pricing data from 50 e-commerce websites for a backtesting exercise bears no resemblance in complexity to a continuous feed monitoring 500+ web sources daily for a live trading signal.

Below is a breakdown of the main pricing tiers Clymin sees across hedge fund and asset management clients.

### Tier 1: Targeted Pilot Projects ($100–$500)

One-time data extractions for backtesting and signal evaluation. Typical scope: one to five data sources, historical extraction covering 6-24 months, delivery as a flat file export. Suitable for quantitative researchers evaluating whether a specific alternative data source contains tradeable signal before committing to ongoing collection.

### Tier 2: Single-Source Monthly Feeds ($500–$2,000/month)

Ongoing collection from a focused source set — for example, daily pricing extraction from 100 e-commerce targets, or weekly job posting extractions from 10 major job boards for a specific industry. Includes automated delivery, quality monitoring, and maintenance when target sites change structure. Most small and emerging manager alt data programs start at this tier.

### Tier 3: Multi-Source Research Platforms ($2,000–$5,000/month)

Comprehensive coverage across 10-30 data sources with daily or near-real-time refresh. Typical clients are mid-size hedge funds with dedicated quant research teams integrating multiple data signals. This tier includes normalized, cross-source schemas, anomaly alerts, and API delivery in addition to file exports.

### Tier 4: Institutional Alt Data Feeds ($5,000–$10,000+/month)

High-volume, high-frequency collection across 50+ sources with sub-daily refresh, dedicated pipeline monitoring, priority support SLAs, and full compliance documentation packages. Suitable for large multi-strategy funds, systematic hedge funds, and asset managers building proprietary ESG or macro data programs.

## What Affects Alternative Data Pricing Most?

### Number of Sources

Each data source requires a dedicated extraction pipeline, schema definition, and maintenance process. Ten sources cost roughly three to four times more to maintain than three sources. Clymin's pricing scales sub-linearly with source count because shared infrastructure reduces per-source overhead.

### Refresh Frequency

Daily batch collection is meaningfully cheaper than hourly or near-real-time extraction. For most fundamental and macro strategies, daily feeds provide sufficient signal freshness. High-frequency and statistical arbitrage strategies may require sub-daily collection, which increases infrastructure requirements and cost.

### Site Complexity and Anti-Bot Measures

Major e-commerce platforms, social networks, and financial portals deploy advanced bot detection that requires sophisticated countermeasures. Sites without aggressive anti-bot measures cost 30-50% less to extract than highly defended targets. Clymin's pricing reflects the actual engineering effort required for each specific target.

### Historical Data Requirements

Backtesting requires historical point-in-time data that was available at each historical date. Building historical archives for new data sources involves web archive access, cached page extraction, and retroactive data reconstruction — all of which carry one-time costs in addition to ongoing feed pricing.
